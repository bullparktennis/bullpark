          ABOUT BULLPARK

          Welcome to BullPark!

          Background

          BullPark was formed by a few tennis addicts that wanted to get their fix throughout the winter months—but only with upbeat, friendly people. BullPark is a portmanteau. It started small, with 12–13 people, but has since grown based largely on the awesomeness of the Frederick Tennis Community. The goal is to have fun and improve your game without the hassle of overly intense competition. BullPark loves connecting people. There are also beverages. And sometimes cookies.

          Core Philosophy

          BullPark only collects positive statistics. Please base all your interactions and energy on that simple concept. If you're not smiling, joking, or laughing at the end of each set—you're doing it wrong.

          BullPark Points & Scoring Rules

          You earn BullPark Points when you win a set with someone. Points are awarded based on how many times you've won with that same teammate.

          • 4 points: Win with a new teammate.
          • 3 points: Win for the second time with the same teammate.
          • 2 points: Win for the third time with the same teammate.
          • 1 point: Attend a BullPark session but win no sets (Sympathy Point).

          Special Exceptions

          • +1 point: Win with Ira (the senior discount).
          • +1 point: Win when Brad is your opponent, in recognition of the stress his lateness causes Eric.
          • +1 point: Earn an Awesomeness Point for going above and beyond to benefit the Frederick Tennis Community or the BullPark group. These bonus points are awarded at the discretion of Chris or Eric.

          Example

          Date Result Points
          Nov. 7 Wins with Eric P (new teammate) +4
          Nov. 14 Wins with Chris B (new teammate) +4
          Nov. 21 Wins with Eric P (second win together) +3
          Nov. 28 Wins with Ira (new teammate + senior discount) +5
          Dec. 5 Wins with Derek J (new teammate + beats Brad) +5
          Total Season Points 21

          How a BullPark Week Works

          1. Sunday at 1 PM: Sign-ups open. Eric will text a reminder at that time.
          2. Sign up: Click the link, open the Sign Ups tab, and enter your first name and last initial. Example: Ira D
          3. First 20 players: The first 20 people to sign up are guaranteed a spot at the following Saturday session.
          4. Wait List: Anyone who signs up after the first 20 is added to the Wait List. Wait-listed players are guaranteed a spot the subsequent week if they don't play.
          5. Wednesday night: Sign-ups close, and starting court assignments are posted.
          6. Saturday at 7:50 PM: Arrive at West Winds on time (hear that, Brad?).
          7. After your first set: You may switch and play with or against anyone you choose for the remainder of the night. If you don't have a preference, Eric, Chris, or another organizer will direct you to a court.
          8. Sunday: BullPark Points are updated.

          A Note About Sign-Ups

          BullPark is shamelessly preferential. We will often add people to the sign-up list before it opens to the group due to extenuating circumstances.

          Examples include:

          • A specific player will be out of town soon.
          • A specific player just made BullPark better in some way.
          • Eric or Chris just want someone to be able to play.
